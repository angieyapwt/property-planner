SOURCES & SCOPE

This is a bank-loan planning estimate, not an approval or an HDB/EC eligibility assessment. It does not test citizenship, household eligibility, income ceilings, remaining lease, CPF withdrawal limits or a lender's full credit policy. Loan amounts are limited by both debt servicing and LTV. Property price is the selected loan divided by its LTV, assuming valuation equals price. It requires the displayed equity contribution and is not a complete cash-budget assessment. Cash downpayments exclude stamp duties, legal fees and other purchase costs.

Assessable income is monthly income + other annual income / 12. Other annual income is always recognised at 70%. Monthly income marked variable is also recognised at 70%; lenders may apply stricter treatment and require supporting documents. Joint age is weighted by gross assessable income. Enter credit-card monthly assessed repayments, not the outstanding balance or credit limit. This workspace assumes no existing housing loans. EC means a purchase subject to MSR; resale EC financing after MOP is outside this simplified assessment.

Automatic OA uses 2026 private-sector employee contribution and allocation tables for Singapore citizens / full-rate PRs, capped at $8,000 ordinary monthly wages. It excludes bonus CPF and assumes retirement contributions continue to RA from age 55. New age bands start in the month after the relevant birthday. Use a manual OA amount for other CPF profiles or years. OA contributions are not an assurance that CPF can fund the full mortgage.

LEASE ASSUMPTIONS & SCOPE

Built year is an estimate of lease commencement, which may differ from the actual lease start. The default original lease is 99 years and may be edited. If the lease reaches age 95, the current LTV applies without pro-rating. A remaining lease of 20 years or less gives 0% under this formula. The calculation uses the unrounded ratio for the maximum loan.

This tool implements the supplied formula as a planning estimate. HDB lease-based loan limits and CPF usage rules are related but are separate from bank-loan assessments. Actual financing also depends on the lower price/valuation, age, tenure, income, lender credit assessment and eligibility; this tool's maximum loan is simply property price × applied pro-rated LTV.
