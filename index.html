SOURCES & SCOPE

This is a bank-loan planning estimate, not an approval or an HDB/EC eligibility assessment. It does not test citizenship, household eligibility, income ceilings, remaining lease, CPF withdrawal limits or a lender's full credit policy. Loan amounts are limited by both debt servicing and LTV. Cash downpayments exclude stamp duties, legal fees and other purchase costs.

Assessable income is monthly income + other annual income / 12. Bank-recognised income applies a 30% reduction to income marked variable; lenders may apply stricter treatment and require supporting documents. Joint age is weighted by gross assessable income. Enter credit-card monthly assessed repayments, not the outstanding balance or credit limit. Existing property-loan instalments count in both TDSR and applicable MSR.

Automatic OA uses 2026 private-sector employee contribution and allocation tables for Singapore citizens / full-rate PRs, capped at $8,000 ordinary monthly wages. It excludes bonus CPF and assumes the indicated FRS setting. New age bands start in the month after the relevant birthday. Use a manual OA amount for other CPF profiles or years. OA contributions are not an assurance that CPF can fund the full mortgage.
